About

My work spans mobile apps and dashboards, from early research to the final interface. I start by understanding who a product is for and what they are trying to do, then shape the flow before polishing the screens. I care about clarity: every element should earn its place, and the whole should feel balanced.

I also use AI tools in my process, from exploring ideas to building quick prototypes. I set the direction, make the decisions and judge the result.
